## 3. Model changes made during QA

1. **Per-customer measures:** Added `Purchasing Customers`, the distinct customers with a sale in the current filter. `NetRevenuePerCustomer`, `GrossProfitPerCustomer`, `NetSalesPerCustomer`, `ReturnIncidence`, and `OrderPerCustomer` now use it. Previously, `Customers` returned 1,000 in every period (June 2026 had 276 buyers), understating per-customer values by about 3.6 times. `Customers` itself is unchanged.
2. **`ActiveCustomers`:** Redefined as customers who bought in the 180 days ending on the last date of the selected period. Previously it was fixed to the model's final date and blank for all of 2025. Total remains 848.
3. **Currency formats:** `GrossSales`, `DiscountValue`, `RefundValue`, and the `ListPrice` and `RefundAmount` columns now display ₦ rather than $.
4. **Targets:** Rebuilt the `FactTargets`–`DimDate` relationship as many-to-many with single-direction filtering from date to targets, replacing a bidirectional relationship. `SalesTarget` and `SalesVariance` return blank when individual dates appear on a visual axis, avoiding display of a full monthly target for each day. Targets still total ₦3,554,057,000.
5. **`AverageDiscount%`:** Changed to a sales-weighted calculation (discount value divided by gross sales): 4.80% overall, previously an unweighted 4.68%.
6. **Customer segments:** Original rules were retained and additional rules added:
   - Five or more orders but revenue below P20: **Frequent Low Value** (61 customers).
   - Two to four orders and last purchase 91–180 days ago: **Cooling Customer** (57 customers).
   - Exactly one order: **Occasional Customer** (now 1 customer).
   - Otherwise: **Unclassified** (currently none).
7. **Housekeeping:** Raw columns (`PaymentMethod`, `ReturnQty`, `ReturnReason`, `Brand`, `City`, `RefundAmount`) hidden. `ShippingDate` removed; fulfillment date tracked through `ShipDate`. `ShippingDate` (if legacy), `DiscountPct`, and `DiscountBandSort` set to no summarization.
8. **Removed measures:** `YoYGrowth%` and, by the report owner, prior-year measures.
9. **Owner changes:** Typos corrected and Auto date/time disabled.

**Target-relationship incident:** The first attempt at a single-direction relationship used the wrong filter direction and showed the full target in every period. Testing caught this immediately; it was reverted, rebuilt correctly, and re-verified.
